Working principle

Good analysis should survive scrutiny — and still make sense to the people who must act on it.

That principle shapes how I approach data quality, methods, visualisation, documentation and interpretation.

I am a health-data and research professional with training across public health, demography, applied analytics and management. My work sits at the intersection of population health, health systems, inequality, business intelligence and evidence-based strategy.

I am especially interested in questions where technical rigour has to connect with practical action: service delivery, patient and population outcomes, access and equity, programme performance, operational intelligence and management decision-making.
