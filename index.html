How I work

Health is the domain. Analytics, data science and AI are the tools. Better decisions are the outcome.

I care about rigorous methods, reproducibility, useful interfaces and the quality of the decision at the end of the workflow.

I am a health-data and research professional working across population health, health systems, inequality, analytics, data science and applied AI. My work increasingly spans the full path from raw data to analytical models, deployed products and decision support.

The portfolio is intentionally multidisciplinary. Health and public health remain the strongest domain anchor, while selected business, commercial and research problems demonstrate the ability to transfer analytical thinking across sectors.
